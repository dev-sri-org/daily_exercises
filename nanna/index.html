Daily Exercises for Amma and Nanna

Gentle, mostly seated or supported routines. Monday to Friday, about 30 minutes a day.

Before you start: safety first
  • Check with the doctor before beginning or changing any exercise routine, especially with atrial fibrillation or if medicines (like blood thinners) have changed.
  • Go at a pace where you can still talk in full sentences. Never hold your breath or strain.
  • Stop and rest if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, unusually short of breath, or notice a racing or irregular heartbeat that is worse than normal.
  • Call for an ambulance for chest pain or pressure, fainting, sudden weakness or trouble speaking.
  • Wear sturdy, non-slip shoes. Keep a firm chair or kitchen counter within reach, and have water nearby. Skip a day if feeling unwell.
